Meatloaf Recipe

Description

A rustic american favorite, the meatloaf is as simple as it gets when it comes to a family dinner paired with mashed potatoes and a vegetable and you'll have the whole family wanting more!

Ingredients

  1. 1 ½ pounds ground beef
  2. 1 large egg
  3. 1 onion, chopped
  4. 1 cup milk
  5. 1 cup dried bread crumbs
  6. salt and pepper to taste
  7. ⅓ cup ketchup
  8. 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  9. 2 tablespoons prepared mustard
